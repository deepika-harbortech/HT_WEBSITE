Overview

Purpose-built for ports and terminals

Container Freight Stations and Port Communities run on paperwork — gate passes, stuffing reports, customs filings, tariff sheets — spread across systems that were never built to talk to each other. Harbor Tech replaces that patchwork with software designed around how cargo, documents, and stakeholders actually move through a port.

Solutions

Two systems. One connected operation.

01 / CFS Solution

Container Freight Station Solution

Manage the full life of cargo through your CFS — from gate-in to stuffing and destuffing, storage, and billing — in one system built around how a freight station actually operates.

  • Gate-in / gate-out tracking with real-time cargo status
  • Stuffing and destuffing workflows tied to storage allocation
  • Configurable tariffs and automated billing
02 / Port Community System

Port Community System (Maritime Single Window)

A single electronic point of contact between shipping lines, customs, port authorities, and other stakeholders — built to meet Maritime Single Window requirements while cutting the paperwork that slows every party down.

  • One filing point for shipping lines, customs, and agents
  • Standards-based electronic data exchange
  • Full visibility across every stakeholder in the chain
